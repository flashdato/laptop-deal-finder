Before you pay

  • MacBook: get the seller to show it's signed out of iCloud. If it's activation-locked, it's worth parts only.
  • ThinkPad and EliteBook: check for a BIOS or supervisor password.
  • Compare the serial sticker with the manufacturer's site to confirm the real spec.
  • On expensive units, ask for the box or receipt. Walk away from sellers who won't meet.
  • Bring a USB-C PD charger, a bootable USB stick and a multimeter.
